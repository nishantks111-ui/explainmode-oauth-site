ExplainMode Automation is a private, locally operated tool used by the owner of the ExplainMode YouTube channel to create, upload, manage and schedule original technology education videos.

Read the Privacy Policy

What the application does

The application helps the channel owner prepare original short-form technology videos, render them locally, and use Google's YouTube APIs to upload and schedule those videos on the owner's own YouTube channel.

  • Creates original technology explainer content.
  • Renders branded vertical videos locally.
  • Uploads approved videos to the owner's YouTube channel.
  • Reads basic channel information to confirm the correct channel.
  • Updates eligible private video metadata or scheduling when instructed by the owner.

Google and YouTube access

ExplainMode Automation uses Google OAuth 2.0 only to operate the owner's own YouTube channel through the YouTube Data API.

OAuth credentials and refresh tokens are stored locally on the owner's computer and are not intentionally published or sold.

Data practices

ExplainMode Automation does not sell Google user data and is not offered as a public service for third-party users.
