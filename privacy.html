Privacy Policy

Effective date: November 17, 2026

Purpose

ExplainMode Automation is a private, locally operated creator workflow used by the owner of the ExplainMode YouTube channel to create, upload, confirm and schedule original YouTube content.

Google user data accessed

The application may access Google/YouTube data to upload videos, read basic authorized channel information, and manage eligible private video metadata or scheduling when explicitly instructed.

How data is used

Google user data is used only to operate the ExplainMode YouTube channel. It is not sold, rented, used to build advertising profiles, or used for unrelated purposes.

Storage

OAuth access and refresh tokens are stored locally on the owner's computer. Generated media, local databases and operational logs are also primarily stored locally.

Sharing

ExplainMode Automation does not intentionally sell, rent or share Google user data with third parties. Data is transmitted to Google/YouTube only as necessary to use the relevant APIs.

Security

OAuth credential files, tokens and environment secrets are excluded from the public source repository and are intended to remain on the owner's local computer.

Retention and deletion

Locally stored tokens and application data may be deleted by the owner at any time. Access may also be revoked from Google Account connected-app controls.

Google API Services User Data Policy

ExplainMode Automation's use and transfer of information received from Google APIs will adhere to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including applicable Limited Use requirements.

Changes

This policy may be updated when functionality or data practices change.

Contact

For questions, use the developer/support contact associated with ExplainMode Automation on its Google OAuth consent screen.
